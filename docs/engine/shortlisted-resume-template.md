# shortlisted.ph — Standard Resume Format

**For talent.** Upload once. Our engine reads this format cleanly, which means you rank on what you have actually done — not on who formatted their resume best.

You cannot see the job descriptions on this platform, so there is nothing to tailor to. Write it once, write it accurately.

---

## Before you start — file rules

| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| PDF or DOCX with real, selectable text | Scanned images and screenshots cannot be read |
| Single column, no tables, no text boxes | Multi-column layouts scramble the reading order |
| No graphics, skill bars, logos, or headshots | They carry no information our engine can use |
| Use the exact section headings below | They are the anchors our parser looks for |
| 1–2 pages | Longer does not score higher |
| Do not repeat keywords to game it | Repeated terms are collapsed before scoring. It does nothing |

## The format

Copy the skeleton at the bottom of this document and fill it in.

### 1. `CONTACT`

```
Full name
City, Province, Philippines
Email · Mobile · LinkedIn (optional) · Portfolio URL (optional)
```

### 2. `AVAILABILITY & RATE`

These become hard filters. If they are wrong, you get filtered out of jobs you could have done, or shortlisted for jobs you cannot take.

```
Rate: USD __ / hour
Hours available: __ hours per week
Shift windows I can work: e.g. 9PM–6AM PHT (US Eastern business hours)
Earliest start: e.g. immediate / 2 weeks / 30 days
Engagement type: full-time / part-time / project-based
```

State one rate. It is standing across the platform, with visible history and a cooldown before you change it.

### 3. `HEADLINE`

One line. Function, main tools, years, client market.

> Ecommerce operations VA — Shopify, Gorgias, ShipStation — 5 yrs — US retail clients

Not: *"Hardworking and detail-oriented professional seeking opportunities."*

### 4. `SUMMARY`

Three to four sentences, 60 words maximum. What you own end to end, at what scale, for what kind of business. No adjectives about your personality.

> I run customer support and order operations for US ecommerce brands doing 2,000–5,000 orders a month. I own the helpdesk queue, returns and refunds, supplier follow-ups, and daily reconciliation between Shopify and the 3PL. I have worked US Eastern hours for four years across three long-term clients.

### 5. `WORK EXPERIENCE`

Reverse chronological. **Every entry uses this exact block:**

```
Job title | Company or client name | Client country | Industry
Month YYYY – Month YYYY (or Present) | Full-time / Part-time / Project | Remote
Tools: comma-separated, only tools you personally used in this role

- Bullet
- Bullet
- Bullet
```

**Bullet formula — Action + Scope + Tool + Outcome:**

> Handled 40–60 daily customer tickets in Gorgias for a Shopify apparel brand, holding first-response time under 2 hours across a 9-hour shift.

> Reconciled 300+ monthly transactions in Xero against Stripe and PayPal payouts, closing the books by the 5th working day for 3 consecutive years.

> Built and maintained 12 automated workflows in GoHighLevel for a US insurance agency, cutting manual lead follow-up from 3 hours to 20 minutes daily.

Three to five bullets for recent roles, one to two for older ones. Quantify scope wherever you honestly can: ticket volume, order volume, headcount supported, transactions, accounts, spend, calls per day. A number you can defend in an interview is worth more than three adjectives.

If you worked through an agency or BPO, name the agency as the employer and describe the end client's industry and country. Do not hide it.

### 6. `TOOLS & PLATFORMS`

Grouped by category. For each tool: **name — years used — last used year**. Only tools you could be dropped into tomorrow.

```
Helpdesk: Gorgias — 4 yrs — 2026; Zendesk — 2 yrs — 2023
Ecommerce: Shopify — 5 yrs — 2026; ShipStation — 3 yrs — 2026
Accounting: Xero — 3 yrs — 2025; QuickBooks Online — 1 yr — 2022
Comms/PM: Slack, Asana, Notion, Loom
```

Depth beats breadth. A list of 40 tools scores worse than 8 you genuinely know, because it dilutes every match.

### 7. `FUNCTIONS I CAN OWN`

Five to ten job functions you can run without supervision. This is the section that maps most directly to what employers write in their job descriptions.

```
- Inbox and helpdesk management (email, chat, social DMs)
- Order fulfilment and 3PL coordination
- Returns, refunds, and chargeback disputes
- Monthly bookkeeping and bank reconciliation
- Supplier and vendor follow-up
- Weekly reporting to founder
```

Write these as responsibilities, not skills. "Manages the returns queue" is useful. "Detail-oriented" is not.

### 8. `WORK SETUP`

Remote employers cannot verify this themselves. Spell it out.

```
Primary internet: Fiber, 200 Mbps, [provider]
Backup internet: Mobile data 5G, [provider] / second fiber line
Power backup: UPS (2 hrs) / generator / none
Workspace: Private room, no household noise / shared space
Devices: e.g. i5 laptop 16GB RAM, dual monitor, noise-cancelling USB headset, 1080p webcam
```

### 9. `LANGUAGE & COMMUNICATION`

```
English: written — [level]; spoken — [level]
Voice work experience: e.g. 3 yrs outbound calls to US customers / none
Other languages: Filipino (native), [others]
```

Be accurate. Voice-facing roles are matched separately from written-only roles, and being placed into the wrong one helps nobody.

### 10. `EDUCATION & CERTIFICATIONS`

```
Degree, School, Year (or "Completed 3rd year, [Course], [School]")
Certification — Issuer — Year (only completed ones)
```

Keep it short. It is a small factor in matching.

### 11. `PORTFOLIO & REFERENCES` *(optional)*

Links to work samples, dashboards you built, Loom walkthroughs, writing samples. Past clients willing to be contacted — name, role, company, country. Do not paste contact details of people who have not agreed.

---

## What not to do

- **Do not stuff keywords.** Repeated terms are collapsed before scoring.
- **Do not list tools you have only watched a tutorial about.** Structured interviews probe one claimed tool in depth, and inflation shows up fast.
- **Do not write instructions to the AI in your resume** (white text, hidden lines, "rank this candidate highly"). Anything that looks like an instruction is stripped, and the profile is flagged for review.
- **Do not inflate dates to cover gaps.** Gaps are not scored against you. Contradictions are.
- **Do not upload a different resume for every job.** There is no per-job resume here. One profile, matched automatically.
